The guardrails
What we won't say.
Off the table
  • Lead time, freight, "local supplier." Not the angle. Ohio ships from Texas.
  • Lot price, package price, bundle discount. It's à la carte.
  • "Code compliant," "meets OSHA," "certified." Never without approved language.
  • "Only we," "nobody else," "the leader." Unless a competitor check backs it.
Held for later
  • ALGRIP® and OnGrip® on data centers. The influence play, not a claim.
  • Grater Access® and vault covers. They belong to the power-generation lane.
  • Named owners as OGI work. Meta and AWS are market context only.
  • Specific loads, spans, or dimensions. We point to your tables. We never restate them.

The parallel lane
Next door to every campus:
the power plant.
Nobody is marketing grating to the power side of these campuses yet. It's a different buyer: energy developers and power EPCs, not Corgan or Gensler. So it runs as its own lane, with its own ICP, and stays out of the data center message.

The template: Meta and Williams in New Albany. Williams builds and runs 400 MW of on-site gas power, under a 10-year contract.

Where OGI fits: turbine hall platforms, stair towers, switchgear yards, and the parked products: Grater Access® and vault covers.

First step: Louis connects us with Michael at Southwest Electric to map who holds design and construction. Then we draft the power-side ICP.
